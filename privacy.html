Privacy Policy

How AZERTYCORP collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

Last updated: 2026

1. Who we are

AZERTYCORP ("we", "us", "our") is a technology and design company based in Douala, Cameroon. We provide website development, software engineering, artificial intelligence, branding, and consulting services. This policy explains how we handle personal data we collect through our website at azertycorp.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to respond to you and to operate our website:

  • Contact information — name, email address, phone number, and any details you share in our contact or quote forms.
  • Usage data — aggregated, anonymous analytics about how visitors use our site (pages visited, approximate location, device type).
  • Cookies — used to remember your preferences and, with your consent, to measure advertising performance.

3. Consent and cookies

When you first visit our site, we ask for your consent before enabling any tracking cookies. We may use the following services, all of which are activated only after you give consent:

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — to understand how visitors use the site.
  • Google Ads — to measure the performance of advertising campaigns.
  • Meta Pixel — to measure the performance of campaigns on Facebook and Instagram.

You can change your choice at any time by clearing your cookies or contacting us. Refusing tracking does not affect your ability to use the site.

4. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and prepare quotes.
  • To deliver and support the services you request.
  • To improve our website and measure our marketing.
  • To comply with our legal and contractual obligations.

5. Forms and third parties

Our contact and quote forms are processed through Web3Forms, a third-party service that forwards submissions to our inbox. By submitting a form you agree that your details will be sent to that service. We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.

6. Data retention

We keep enquiry data only as long as needed to respond to you and to manage the resulting relationship. Analytics data is retained in aggregated form only.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to withdraw any consent you gave. To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@azertycorp.com.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information to the best of our ability.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@azertycorp.com, call +237 687 391 985, or write to us in Douala, Cameroon.
